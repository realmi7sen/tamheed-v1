## Questions

### Question 1
(2+3+2)

1. Find $\displaystyle\int\frac{(1+x^{\frac{2}{3}})^{4}}{x^{\frac{1}{3}}}\, dx.$
2. Use Riemann sums to find $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{2}(x^{2}+4)\, dx.$
3. Find $\displaystyle\int\frac{\ln x+1}{\sqrt{9-x^{2}(\ln x)^{2}}}\, dx.$

### Question 2
(3+3+3)

1. Find $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{1-x^{8}}}.$
2. Compute $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow+\infty}(1+e^{-2x})^{e^{x}}$
3. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int x \sec^{2}x\, dx.$

### Question 3
(3+3+3)

1. Compute the integral $\displaystyle\int \sin^{2}x \cos^{5}x\, dx.$
2. Find $\displaystyle\int\frac{x+3}{(x^{2}+9)^{\frac{3}{2}}}\, dx.$
3. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{x^{3}+3}{(x+1)(x^{2}+1)}\, dx.$

### Question 4
(3+3+3)

1. Find $\displaystyle\int_{3}^{+\infty}\frac{dx}{x(\ln x)^{3}}$
2. Sketch the region bounded by the curves $y=\sqrt{x+6},$ the $x$-axis, $y=x,$ and find its area.
3. Find the volume of the solid obtained by revolving the region bounded by $y=(x-2)^{2},$ $y=1$ about the $y$-axis.

### Question 5
(3+3)

1. Find the surface area obtained by revolving the curve $x=t^{3},$ $y=3t+1,$ $0\le t \le 1$ about the $y$-axis.
2. Sketch the region inside $r=3 \sin \theta$ and outside $r=3-3 \sin \theta$ and find its area.
