## Questions

### Question 1
(2+2+3)

a) Use Trapezoid rule with $n=5$ to approximate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{5}\frac{dx}{1+x^{3}}.$

b) Find the number(s) $c$ that satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem for $f(x)=(x-2)^{2}$ on $[-1,5]$.

c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int x\sin(x^{2})3^{\cos(x^{2})}\,dx.$

### Question 2
(3+3+3)

a) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{x^{4}-1}}.$

b) Compute the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{\cot x\,dx}{\sqrt{1-\sin^{4}x}}.$

c) Find the indefinite integral $\displaystyle\int \cosh^{-1}x\,dx.$

### Question 3
(3+3+3)

a) Compute the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{(x^{2}+4)^{2}}.$

b) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x^{\frac{1}{2}}-x^{\frac{1}{3}}}.$

c) Find $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{5+3\cos x+4\sin x}.$

### Question 4
(3+3+3)

a) Does the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{+\infty}(1+x)e^{-2x}\,dx$ converge? Find its value if it does.

b) Sketch the region bounded by the curves $y=(x-1)^{2}$, $y=(x+1)^{2}$, $y=0$ and find its area.

c) Find the volume obtained by revolving the region bounded by $y=x^{2}$ and $y=\sqrt{x}$ about the $y$-axis.

### Question 5
(3+3)

a) Compute the area of the surface of revolution obtained by revolving the parametric curve $x=-2+2t^{2}$, $y=4t$, $0\le t\le1$ about the $x$-axis.

b) Sketch the region inside the curve $r=1+\sin\theta$ and outside $r=1-\sin\theta$ and find its area.
