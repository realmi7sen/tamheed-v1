## Questions

### Question 1
Evaluate the following integrals:

a) $\displaystyle\int\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{1+x^{3}}}\, dx$

b) $\displaystyle\int x e^{x}\, dx$

c) $\displaystyle\int \cos^{3}x\, dx$

d) $\displaystyle\int \tan^{2}x\, dx$

e) $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x^{2}\sqrt{16-x^{2}}}$

f) $\displaystyle\int\frac{x}{x^{2}-4x+8}\, dx$

g) $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{(x-1)(x+2)}$

### Question 2

i) Find the following limit: $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow 0^{+}}(\tan x)^{\tan x}$

ii) Show that the integral $\displaystyle\int_{1}^{+\infty}\frac{\ln x}{x^{2}}\,dx$ converges and find its value.

### Question 3

a) Sketch the region R bounded by the curves $y=2-x^{2}$, $y=x$ and find its area.

b) Find the volume of the solid generated by revolving the region bounded by the curves: $y=x^{2}$ and $y=2x$ about the $x$-axis.

c) Determine the length of the curve $y=1+\frac{2}{3}x^{3/2}$ from $x=0$ to $x=3$.

d) Sketch the region R that lies inside the curve $r=2\sin\theta$, the curve $r=2-2\sin\theta$, and find its area.
