## Questions

### Question 1
(2+3+3+3)

a) Set $\displaystyle F(x)=x^{3}\int_{0}^{x}\sqrt{2+\sin t}\,dt$. Find $F'(0)$.

b) Use the substitution $u=x^{2}+1$ to compute $\displaystyle\int\frac{x^{3}}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}\,dx$

c) Compute the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{e^{2x}-25}}\,dx$

d) Find $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow0^{+}}\sin x \cdot \ln(\sin x)$

### Question 2
(3+3+3+2+3)

a) Find $\displaystyle\int x(\sec x)^{2}\,dx$

b) Compute the indefinite integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{(1+x^{2})^{2}}\,dx$

c) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{x+1}{x(x^{2}+4)}\,dx$

d) $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^{2}-4x}}\,dx$

e) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{x^{3/2}+x^{1/2}}\,dx$

### Question 3
(3+3+3+3+3)

a) Determine whether the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{x}}{e^{2x}+1}\,dx$ converges or diverges and find its value if it converges.

b) Sketch the region bounded by the curves $y=x^{2}+5$ and $y=2x+8$ and find its area.

c) Find the volume obtained by revolving the region bounded by the curves $y=x^{2}$ and $y=\sqrt{8x}$ about the $y$-axis.

d) Find the arc length of the parametric curve given by $x=\frac{t^{5}}{5}$, $y=\frac{2}{15}t^{15/2}$, $0\le t\le1$.

e) Sketch the polar curve $r=2+2\cos\theta$, $0\le\theta\le2\pi$, and find the area of the region inside this curve.
