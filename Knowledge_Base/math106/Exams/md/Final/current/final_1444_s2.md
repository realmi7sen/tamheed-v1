## Questions

### Question 1

1. Let $\displaystyle F(x) = \int_0^{-x^2} \cos\sqrt{t^2+1}\, dt$. Find $F^{\prime}(x)$.
2. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x((\ln x)^{2}+9)}$
3. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{1-x^{6}}}$

### Question 2

1. Evaluate the limit $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow0^{+}}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^{x^{2}}$
2. Find the indefinite integral $\displaystyle\int x^{4}\ln x\,dx$
3. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int(\sin x)^{5}(\cos x)^{6}\,dx$

### Question 3

1. Compute the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{(16-x^{2})^{\frac{3}{2}}}$
2. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{x^{2}+1}{x^{3}-x}\,dx$
3. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}+\frac{4}{\sqrt[4]{x}}}\,dx$

### Question 4

1. Determine if the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x}(1+x)}$ converges. Find its value if it does.
2. Sketch the region bounded by the curves $y=(x-1)^{2}$ and $y=3-x$, and find its area.
3. Find the volume of the solid generated by revolving the region bounded by the curves $x=4-y^{2}$ and $x=y^{2}+2$ about the line $x=-1$.

### Question 5

1. Determine the arc length of the curve $x=y+\frac{y^{3}}{3}$ from $y=0$ to $y=1$.
2. Sketch the region inside the polar curve $r=2+2\cos\theta$ and outside the polar curve $r=2$, and find its area.
