## Questions

### Question 1
(2+3+2)

1. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{\sinh(\ln x)}{x}\,dx.$
2. Find the number $c$ in the mean value theorem for the function $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x+1}}$ on $[3,8]$.
3. Compute $\displaystyle\int\frac{\text{sech}^{2}x}{4+\tanh^{2}x}\,dx.$

### Question 2
(3+3+3)

1. Find the indefinite integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{1-x^{6}}}.$
2. Compute $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow+\infty}(1+e^{x})^{e^{-x}}$
3. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int e^{2x}\sin x\,dx.$

### Question 3
(3+3+3)

1. Compute $\displaystyle\int \tan^{2}x\sec^{6}x\,dx.$
2. Find the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{(x^{2}+4)^{2}}.$
3. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{2x-1}{x^{2}+2x+2}\,dx.$

### Question 4
(3+3+2)

1. Sketch the region bounded by $y=x^{2}+1$, $y=3x+1$ and find its area.
2. Find the volume of the solid obtained by revolving the region bounded by the curves $x=y^{2}+1$, $x=10$ about the $y$-axis.
3. Find the arc length of the curve given by $y=2x^{\frac{3}{2}}+1$, $0\le x\le1$.

### Question 5
(3+4)

1. Find the area of the surface obtained by revolving the curve $y=\frac{1}{2}x^{2}$, $0\le x\le1$ about the $y$-axis.
2. Sketch the region inside $r=3+3\sin\theta$ and outside $r=3$ and find its area.
