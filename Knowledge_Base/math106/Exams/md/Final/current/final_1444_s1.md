## Questions

### Question 1
(3+3+3)

a) Use the substitution $u=x^{2}+4$ to compute $\displaystyle\int x^{3}\sqrt{x^{2}+4}\,dx$.

b) Find the number(s) $c$ that satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem for the function $f(x)=-x^{2}+4x$ on $[0,3]$.

c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{e^{4x}-16}}\,dx$

### Question 2
(2+3+3)

a) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{\sinh x}{\sqrt{9+(\cosh x)^{2}}}\,dx$

b) Find the indefinite integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{\ln x}{x^{5}}\,dx$

c) Compute $\displaystyle\int(\sec x)^{6}\,dx$

### Question 3
(3+3+2)

a) Find $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow0}(1+8x^{2})^{\frac{1}{x^{2}}}$

b) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{8x+32}{(x-2)(x^{2}-4)}\,dx$

c) Compute $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^{2}-4x+2}}\,dx$

### Question 4
(3+3+3)

a) Sketch the region bounded by $y=\sqrt{x+2},$ $y=-x$, and the $x$-axis and find its area.

b) Find the volume of the solid obtained by revolving the region bounded by $y=(x-1)^{2}$ and $y=3-x$ about the $x$-axis.

c) Compute the area of the surface obtained by revolving the curve $y=\sqrt{9-x^{2}}$, $-2\le x\le2$ about the $x$-axis.

### Question 5
(3+3)

a) Find the arc length of the parametric curve given by $x=\frac{t^{4}}{4}$, $y=\frac{t^{6}}{6}$, $0\le t\le1$.

b) Sketch the region inside $r=2$ and outside $r=4-4\cos\theta$ and find its area.
