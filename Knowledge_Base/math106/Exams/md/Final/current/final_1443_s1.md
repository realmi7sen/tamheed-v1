## Questions

### Question 1
(2+2)

a) If $\displaystyle F(x)=\int_{x^{2}}^{\sin x}\cosh t\,dt,$ find $F^{\prime}(0)$.

b) Find the number(s) $c$ in the mean value theorem for $f(x)=\frac{1}{x^{2}}$ on $[1,2]$.

### Question 2
(3+3+3)

a) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{3^{2x}-1}}.$

b) Compute $\displaystyle\int\frac{\cot x}{\sqrt{1-\sin^{2}x}}\,dx.$

c) Find $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow0^{+}}x^{\sin x}.$

### Question 3
(3+3+3)

a) Compute the integral $\displaystyle\int x\tan^{-1}x\,dx.$

b) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{(1+x^{2})^{2}}.$

c) Find $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^{2}+8x+25}}.$

### Question 4
(3+3+3)

a) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x^{\frac{1}{4}}+x^{\frac{1}{2}}}.$

b) Does the integral $\displaystyle\int_{2}^{+\infty}\frac{dx}{x(\ln x)^{3}}$ converge? Find its value if it does.

c) Sketch the region bounded by the curves $y=x^{2}+1$, $y=-x^{2}+1$, $x=2$ and find its area.

### Question 5
(3+3+3)

a) Find the volume obtained by revolving the region in the first quadrant bounded by $y=4-x^{2}$, $y=0$ and $x=0$ about the $y$-axis.

b) Find the surface area obtained by revolving the parametric curve $x=t^{3}$, $y=2t+3$, $0\le t\le1$, about the $y$-axis.

c) Sketch the region inside $r=1+\cos\theta$ and outside $r=1-\cos\theta$ and find its area.
