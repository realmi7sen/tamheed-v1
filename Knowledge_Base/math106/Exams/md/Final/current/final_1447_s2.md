## Questions

### Question 1
(3+2+3)

a) Find the number(s) $c$ that satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem for the function $f(x)=2+3\sqrt{x}$ on $[1,4]$.

b) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\ln x-x}$

c) Use Riemann sums to compute $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{2}4-x^{2}\,dx$

### Question 2
(3+3+3)

a) Find $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow+\infty}(1+e^{x})^{\frac{1}{x}}$

b) Find the indefinite integral $\displaystyle\int\sqrt{x}(\ln x)^{2}\,dx$

c) Compute $\displaystyle\int(\tan x)^{5}(\sec x)^{8}\,dx$

### Question 3
(3+3+2)

a) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{x^{2}\sqrt{4+x^{2}}}\,dx$

b) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{3x-1}{x^{2}+4x+8}\,dx$

c) Compute $\displaystyle\int(\sin 5x)\cos x\,dx$

### Question 4
(3+3+3)

a) Does the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{\cos x}{\sqrt{\sin x}}\,dx$ converge? Find its value if it does.

b) Sketch the region bounded by $x=y^{2}$ and $x-y=2$, and find its area.

c) Find the volume of the solid obtained by revolving the region bounded by $y=(x-1)^{2}$, $y=0$, $x=1$, $x=2$ about the $y$-axis.

### Question 5
(3+3)

a) Find the area of the surface obtained by revolving the curve $y=2\sqrt{x+1}$, $0\le x\le3$ about the $x$-axis.

b) Sketch the region inside $r=2+2\cos\theta$ and outside $r=2-2\cos\theta$ and find its area.
