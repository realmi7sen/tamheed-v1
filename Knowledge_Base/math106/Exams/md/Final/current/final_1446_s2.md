## Questions

### Question 1
(3+3+2)

a) Use the substitution $u=1+x^{2}$ to compute $\displaystyle\int x^{5}\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\, dx$

b) Find the number(s) $c$ that satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem for the function $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{(x+1)^{2}}$ on $[0, 3]$.

c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{4-(\ln x)^{2}}}$

### Question 2
(3+3+3)

a) Find the indefinite integral $\displaystyle\int x(\cos x)^{2}\, dx$

b) Compute $\displaystyle\int(\cos x)^{5}(\sin x)^{6}\, dx$

c) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{x^{2}}{\sqrt{25-x^{2}}}\, dx$

### Question 3
(3+3+2)

a) Find $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow0^{+}}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^{x^{2}}$

b) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{x+4}{x(x+1)^{2}}\, dx$

c) Compute $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+3}}\, dx$

### Question 4
(3+3+3)

a) Does the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}xe^{-2x^{2}}\, dx$ converge? Find its value if it does.

b) Sketch the region bounded by $y=x^{2}$, $y=2x+3$, and find its area.

c) Find the volume of the solid obtained by revolving the region bounded by $y=e^{2x}$, $y=0$, $x=0$, $x=1$ about the $y$-axis.

### Question 5
(3+3)

a) Find the arc length of the parametric curve $x=\sin t-t\cos t$, $y=\cos t+t\sin t$, $0\le t\le2$.

b) Sketch the region inside $r=2+2\sin\theta$ and outside $r=2-2\sin\theta$ and find its area.
