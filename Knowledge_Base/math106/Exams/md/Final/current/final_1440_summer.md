## Questions

### Question 1
(3+3)

1. Let $f(x)=(1+\cos x)^{2x}$. Calculate $f^{\prime}(0)$.
2. Find $g(x)$ if $\displaystyle\int e^{x^{2}}g(x)\,dx=-e^{x^{2}}+c,$ where $c$ is a constant.

### Question 2
(2+2+2) Evaluate the following integrals:

$\displaystyle\int(2^{x}+2^{-x}+1)\,dx$, $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{e^{2x}-1}}\,dx$, $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}\sqrt{x+4}}\,dx$

### Question 3
(2+2+3) Evaluate the following integrals:

$\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{x\sqrt{1-x^{8}}}\,dx,$ $\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{(25-x^{2})^{\frac{3}{2}}}\,dx,$ $\displaystyle\int\frac{x^{2}+12x+3}{x^{3}-4x}\,dx$

### Question 4
(3+3+3)

1. Determine if the following integral is convergent or divergent. If it is convergent, find its value: $\displaystyle\int_{1}^{+\infty}\frac{\ln x}{x}\,dx.$
2. Find the area bounded by the graphs of the curves of $y=x^{2}+1$, $y=2x$ and $x=0$.
3. Find the volume of the solid of revolution generated by revolving the region bounded by the graphs of the curves $y=x^{2}$ and $y=2-x^{2}$ around the $x$-axis.

### Question 5
(3+3+3+3)

1. Find the arc length of the curve: $y=\cosh x$ for $0\le x\le4.$
2. Find the points on the polar curve: $r(\theta)=2\cos\theta$, $0\le\theta\le\pi$ at which the tangent line to $r$ is vertical.
3. Find the area of the region bounded by the polar curves $r=\tan\theta$, $\theta=0$ and $\theta=\frac{\pi}{4}$.
4. Find the surface area generated by revolving the polar curve: $r=\cos\theta,$ $-\frac{\pi}{2}\le\theta\le\frac{\pi}{2}$ around the $y$-axis.
