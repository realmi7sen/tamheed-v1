## Questions

### Question 1

1. Approximate the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{4}\sqrt{x^{3}+8}\,dx$ using Simpson's Rule with $n=4$.
2. Find $F^{\prime}(x)$ if $F(x) = (2+\sin x)^{e^{x}}$.

### Question 2

1. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int(3^{x}+3^{-x}+2)\,dx$.
2. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{2^{2x}-1}}$.
3. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x}\sqrt{1+x}}$.

### Question 3

1. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{4-x^{6}}}$.
2. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{(x^{2}-1)^{\frac{3}{2}}}$.
3. Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{4x^{2}}{(x-1)^{2}(x+1)}\,dx$.

### Question 4

1. Determine if the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{+\infty}(1+2x)e^{-x}\,dx$ converges or diverges. Find its value if it converges.
2. Sketch the region bounded by the curves $y=(x-1)^{2}$ and $y=3-x$, and find its area.
3. Find the volume of the solid generated by revolving the region bounded by the curves $x=y^{2}+2$ and $x=4-y^{2}$ about the line $x=-1$.

### Question 5

1. Find the arc length of the curve parameterized by $x(t)$ and $y(t)$ such that $\sqrt{\left(\frac{dx}{dt}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{dy}{dt}\right)^2} = t^2\sqrt{1+t^3}$ on the interval $0 \le t \le 1$.
2. Sketch the polar curve $r=3(1+\cos\theta)$ for $\pi/2 \le \theta \le \pi$, and evaluate the area of the bounded region.
3. Find the area of the surface generated by revolving the polar curve $r=8\cos\theta$ from $\theta=0$ to $\theta=\pi/2$ about the polar axis.
