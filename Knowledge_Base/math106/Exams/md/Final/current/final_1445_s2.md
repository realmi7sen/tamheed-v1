## Questions

### Question 1
(2+1, 2+1, 2+1)

a) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int e^{-3x}\tan(e^{-3x})\,dx$

b) Find the number $c$ that satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem for integrals for the function $f(x)=3(x-1)^{2}$ on $[1,4]$.

c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{x^{4}-9}}$

### Question 2
(2+1, 1.5+1.5, 1.5+1.5)

a) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{1-x^{5}}}$

b) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int x^{2}\cosh x\,dx$

c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int(\sin x)^{5}(\cos x)^{8}\,dx$

### Question 3
(2.5+0.5, 1+1, 1+1)

a) Find the limit $\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow0^{+}}(e^{x}+3x)^{1/x}$

b) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{6x-11}{(x-1)^{2}}\,dx$

c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{7-x^{2}+6x}}$

### Question 4
(2+1, 2, 3)

a) Determine whether the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{\cos x}{\sqrt{1-\sin x}}\,dx$ converges or diverges. Find its value if it converges.

b) Sketch the region bounded by $y=4-x^{2}$ and $y=2x+4$, and find its area.

c) Find the volume of the solid generated by revolving the region bounded by $y=2\sinh x$, $y=0$, $x=0$, and $x=1$ about the $y$-axis.

### Question 5
(3, 2)

a) Find the arc length of the parametric curve defined by $x=e^{t}\cos t$, $y=e^{t}\sin t$ for $0\le t\le \pi/2$.

b) Sketch the region inside $r=2-2\cos\theta$ and outside $r=2\cos\theta$ and find its area.
