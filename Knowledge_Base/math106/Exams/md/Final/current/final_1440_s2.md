## Questions

### Question 1
(2+2)

a) Approximate the integral $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{6}\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\,dx$ using Simpson's Rule with $n=6$.

b) Find the number $c$ in the mean value theorem for $f(x)=\frac{8}{x^{2}}$ on $[2, 4]$.

### Question 2
(3+3+3)

a) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{e^{6x}-25}}$

b) Compute the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{1-x^{8}}}$

c) Find $\displaystyle\int x\tan^{-1}(x)\,dx$

### Question 3
(3+3+3)

a) Compute the following integral $\displaystyle\int(\tan x)^{5}(\sec x)^{3}\,dx$

b) Find the integral $\displaystyle\int \cos(7x)\cos(5x)\,dx$

c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{(x^{2}-1)^{3/2}}$

### Question 4
(3+3+3)

a) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int\frac{2x-1}{x^{2}+4x+20}\,dx$

b) Sketch the region bounded by the curves: $y=2-x^{2}$, $y=x$, $x=0$, $x=2$ and find its area.

c) Set up an integral for the volume obtained by revolving the region bounded by the curves $y=x^{2}$, $y=4$ about the line of equation
i) $y=6$
ii) $x=-3$

### Question 5
(3+3+3)

a) Sketch the region $R$ that lies inside the curve $r=2\sin\theta$ and outside the curve $r=2-2\sin\theta$, and find its area.

b) Find the area of the surface obtained by revolving the curve $r=4\cos\theta$, $0\le\theta\le\pi/2$ about the $y$-axis.

c) Find the length of the curve given by the equations $x=\frac{t^{4}}{4}$, $y=\frac{t^{6}}{6}$, $0\le t\le1$.
