## Definitions
* **Definition 5.11**
  Suppose $f$ is a continuous function where $f(x) \ge 0$ on the interval $[a, b]$, with $0 \le a \le b$. If $R$ represents the region under the graph of $f$ from $a$ to $b$, the volume $V$ of the solid of revolution formed by revolving $R$ about the y-axis is defined as a limit of Riemann sums of cylindrical shells[cite: 3]:
  $$V = \lim_{||P||\to 0} \sum_k 2\pi w_k f(w_k) \Delta x_k = \int_{a}^{b} 2\pi x f(x) dx$$

## Formulas
* **Volume $V$ of a Cylindrical Shell 5.10**
  The volume of a cylindrical shell can be calculated using the product of its average circumference, altitude, and thickness[cite: 3]:
  $$V = 2\pi \cdot (\text{average radius}) \cdot (\text{altitude}) \cdot (\text{thickness})$$

## Guidelines / Methods
* **Guidelines for Finding the Volume of a Solid of Revolution Using Cylindrical Shells 5.12**[cite: 3]
  1. Sketch the region $R$ to be revolved, and label the boundaries. Show a typical vertical rectangle of width $dx$ or a horizontal rectangle of width $dy$[cite: 3].
  2. Sketch the cylindrical shell generated by the rectangle in guideline (1)[cite: 3].
  3. Express the average radius of the shell in terms of $x$ or $y$, depending on whether its thickness is $dx$ or $dy$. Remember that $x$ represents a distance from the y-axis to a vertical rectangle, and $y$ represents a distance from the x-axis to a horizontal rectangle[cite: 3].
  4. Express the altitude of the shell in terms of $x$ or $y$, depending on whether its thickness is $dx$ or $dy$[cite: 3].
  5. Use (5.10) to find a formula for the volume of the shell[cite: 3].
  6. Apply the limit of sums operator $\int_a^b$ or $\int_c^d$ to the expression in guideline (5) and evaluate the integral[cite: 3].

## Worked Examples
### Example 1
The region bounded by the graph of $y = 2x - x^2$ and the x-axis is revolved about the y-axis[cite: 3]. Find the volume of the resulting solid.

**Solution**
* **Step 1:** The region is bounded by $y = 2x - x^2$ and the x-axis, spanning from $x = 0$ to $x = 2$. We sketch a typical vertical rectangle of width $dx$[cite: 3].
* **Step 2:** Revolving this rectangle about the y-axis generates a cylindrical shell[cite: 3].
* **Step 3:** The parameters of the shell are as follows[cite: 3]:
  * thickness of shell: $dx$
  * average radius: $x$
  * altitude: $2x - x^2$
  * volume of shell: $2\pi x(2x - x^2)dx$
* **Step 4:** Integrate from $x = 0$ to $x = 2$[cite: 3]:
  $$V = \int_{0}^{2} 2\pi x(2x - x^2) dx = 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} (2x^2 - x^3) dx$$
  $$= 2\pi \left[ 2\left(\frac{x^3}{3}\right) - \frac{x^4}{4} \right]_0^2 = \frac{8\pi}{3} \approx 8.4$$[cite: 3]

### Example 2
The region bounded by the graphs of $y = x^2$ and $y = x + 2$ is revolved about the line $x = 3$. Set up the integral for the volume of the resulting solid[cite: 3].

**Solution**
* **Step 1:** The region spans from $x = -1$ to $x = 2$. A typical vertical rectangle extends from the lower boundary $y = x^2$ to the upper boundary $y = x + 2$[cite: 3].
* **Step 2:** Revolving about $x = 3$ creates a cylindrical shell[cite: 3].
* **Step 3:** Note the shell parameters[cite: 3]:
  * thickness of shell: $dx$
  * average radius: $3 - x$
  * altitude: $(x + 2) - x^2$
  * volume: $2\pi(3 - x)(x + 2 - x^2)dx$
* **Step 4:** The integral sum over the region is[cite: 3]:
  $$V = \int_{-1}^{2} 2\pi(3 - x)(x + 2 - x^2) dx$$[cite: 3]

### Example 3
The region in the first quadrant bounded by the graph of the equation $x = 2y^3 - y^4$ and the y-axis is revolved about the x-axis. Set up the integral for the volume of the resulting solid[cite: 3].

**Solution**
* **Step 1:** A typical horizontal rectangle of width $dy$ is drawn from the y-axis to the curve[cite: 3].
* **Step 2:** Revolving about the x-axis generates a cylindrical shell[cite: 3].
* **Step 3:** Identify the components of the shell[cite: 3]:
  * thickness of shell: $dy$
  * average radius: $y$
  * altitude: $2y^3 - y^4$
  * volume: $2\pi y(2y^3 - y^4)dy$
* **Step 4:** To sum the shells, we integrate from $y = 0$ to $y = 2$[cite: 3]:
  $$V = \int_{0}^{2} 2\pi y(2y^3 - y^4) dy$$[cite: 3]
