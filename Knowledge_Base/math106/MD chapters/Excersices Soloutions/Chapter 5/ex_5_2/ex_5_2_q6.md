## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the indicated axis.
$y = \sqrt{x}$, $x = 4$, $y = 0$; x-axis

## Rule Used
Volume $V$ of a Circular Disk 5.7

## Solution
**Step 1 — Identify the components of the disk**
The region is revolved around the x-axis. The cross-sections are circular disks.
The radius of the disk is the function value: $\text{radius} = \sqrt{x}$.
The thickness is $dx$.
The limits of integration are from $x = 0$ (the origin, where $y=\sqrt{x}$ intersects $y=0$) to $x = 4$.

**Step 2 — Set up the integral for volume**
$$V = \int_{0}^{4} \pi [f(x)]^2 dx$$
$$V = \int_{0}^{4} \pi (\sqrt{x})^2 dx$$
$$V = \pi \int_{0}^{4} x dx$$

**Step 3 — Evaluate the definite integral**
$$V = \pi \left[ \frac{x^2}{2} \right]_0^4$$
$$V = \pi \left( \frac{4^2}{2} - \frac{0^2}{2} \right)$$
$$V = \pi \left( \frac{16}{2} \right) = 8\pi$$

## Answer
$8\pi$

## Check by Differentiation
$\frac{d}{dx} \left[ \pi \frac{x^2}{2} \right] = \pi x = \pi (\sqrt{x})^2$. Verified.
