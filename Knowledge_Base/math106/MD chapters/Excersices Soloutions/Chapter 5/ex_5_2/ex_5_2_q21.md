## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the indicated axis.
$y = \sin 2x$, $x = 0$, $x = \pi$, $y = 0$; x-axis. (Hint: Use a half-angle formula.)

## Rule Used
Volume $V$ of a Circular Disk 5.7

## Solution
**Step 1 — Identify the components of the disk**
The region $R$ exists between $x = 0$ and $x = \pi$. The radius of revolution about the x-axis is $f(x) = \sin 2x$.
The thickness of the disk is $dx$.

**Step 2 — Set up the integral for volume**
$$V = \int_{0}^{\pi} \pi (\sin 2x)^2 dx$$
$$V = \pi \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin^2 2x dx$$

**Step 3 — Apply the half-angle formula**
The half-angle identity is $\sin^2 \theta = \frac{1 - \cos 2\theta}{2}$. Let $\theta = 2x$, which yields $\sin^2 2x = \frac{1 - \cos 4x}{2}$.
$$V = \pi \int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{1 - \cos 4x}{2} dx = \frac{\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\pi} (1 - \cos 4x) dx$$

**Step 4 — Evaluate the definite integral**
$$V = \frac{\pi}{2} \left[ x - \frac{1}{4}\sin 4x \right]_0^\pi$$
$$V = \frac{\pi}{2} \left( \left(\pi - \frac{1}{4}\sin(4\pi)\right) - \left(0 - \frac{1}{4}\sin(0)\right) \right)$$
Since $\sin(4\pi) = 0$ and $\sin(0) = 0$:
$$V = \frac{\pi}{2} ( \pi - 0 ) = \frac{\pi^2}{2}$$

## Answer
$\frac{\pi^2}{2}$

## Check by Differentiation
$\frac{d}{dx} \left[ \frac{\pi}{2} \left( x - \frac{1}{4}\sin 4x \right) \right] = \frac{\pi}{2} (1 - \cos 4x) = \pi \sin^2 2x$. Verified.
