## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the given line.
$y = x^2$, $y = 4$
(a) $y = 4$
(b) $y = 5$
(c) $x = 2$
(d) $x = 3$

## Rule Used
Volume $V$ of a Circular Disk 5.7 / Volume $V$ of a Washer 5.9

## Solution
**Step 1 — Understand the Region**
The intersection of $y = x^2$ and $y = 4$ yields $x^2 = 4 \implies x = \pm 2$.
The region $R$ is bounded by the parabola $y = x^2$ below and the line $y = 4$ above, spanning $x = -2$ to $x = 2$.

**Part (a) — Revolve about $y = 4$**
The axis of revolution is the upper boundary of the region. We use the disk method with respect to $x$.
*   Radius: Distance from the curve $y = x^2$ to the axis $y = 4$, which is $(4 - x^2)$.
*   Integral: 
    $$V = \int_{-2}^{2} \pi (4 - x^2)^2 dx$$
    $$V = \pi \int_{-2}^{2} (16 - 8x^2 + x^4) dx$$
*   Evaluate:
    $$V = 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} (16 - 8x^2 + x^4) dx \text{ (by symmetry)}$$
    $$V = 2\pi \left[ 16x - \frac{8}{3}x^3 + \frac{x^5}{5} \right]_0^2$$
    $$V = 2\pi \left( 32 - \frac{64}{3} + \frac{32}{5} \right) = 2\pi \left( \frac{480 - 320 + 96}{15} \right) = 2\pi \left( \frac{256}{15} \right) = \frac{512\pi}{15}$$

**Part (b) — Revolve about $y = 5$**
The axis of revolution is above the region. We use the washer method with respect to $x$.
*   Outer Radius: Distance from $y = x^2$ to $y = 5 \implies (5 - x^2)$.
*   Inner Radius: Distance from $y = 4$ to $y = 5 \implies (5 - 4) = 1$.
*   Integral:
    $$V = \int_{-2}^{2} \pi \left[ (5 - x^2)^2 - 1^2 \right] dx$$
    $$V = \pi \int_{-2}^{2} (25 - 10x^2 + x^4 - 1) dx = \pi \int_{-2}^{2} (24 - 10x^2 + x^4) dx$$
*   Evaluate (using symmetry):
    $$V = 2\pi \left[ 24x - \frac{10}{3}x^3 + \frac{x^5}{5} \right]_0^2$$
    $$V = 2\pi \left( 48 - \frac{80}{3} + \frac{32}{5} \right) = 2\pi \left( \frac{720 - 400 + 96}{15} \right) = 2\pi \left( \frac{416}{15} \right) = \frac{832\pi}{15}$$

**Part (c) — Revolve about $x = 2$**
The axis of revolution is the rightmost boundary of the region. We use the washer method with respect to $y$ (horizontal slices).
*   Solve $y = x^2$ for $x$: The right boundary curve is $x = \sqrt{y}$ and the left is $x = -\sqrt{y}$.
*   Outer Radius: Distance from $x = -\sqrt{y}$ to $x = 2 \implies 2 - (-\sqrt{y}) = 2 + \sqrt{y}$.
*   Inner Radius: Distance from $x = \sqrt{y}$ to $x = 2 \implies 2 - \sqrt{y}$.
*   Integral: Bounds are $y = 0$ to $y = 4$.
    $$V = \int_{0}^{4} \pi \left[ (2 + \sqrt{y})^2 - (2 - \sqrt{y})^2 \right] dy$$
    $$V = \pi \int_{0}^{4} \left[ (4 + 4\sqrt{y} + y) - (4 - 4\sqrt{y} + y) \right] dy = \pi \int_{0}^{4} 8\sqrt{y} dy$$
*   Evaluate:
    $$V = 8\pi \left[ \frac{2}{3} y^{3/2} \right]_0^4 = 8\pi \left( \frac{2}{3}(4)^{3/2} \right) = 8\pi \left( \frac{2}{3} \cdot 8 \right) = \frac{128\pi}{3}$$

**Part (d) — Revolve about $x = 3$**
The axis of revolution is a vertical line to the right of the region. We use the washer method with respect to $y$.
*   Outer Radius: Distance from left curve $x = -\sqrt{y}$ to $x = 3 \implies 3 - (-\sqrt{y}) = 3 + \sqrt{y}$.
*   Inner Radius: Distance from right curve $x = \sqrt{y}$ to $x = 3 \implies 3 - \sqrt{y}$.
*   Integral:
    $$V = \int_{0}^{4} \pi \left[ (3 + \sqrt{y})^2 - (3 - \sqrt{y})^2 \right] dy$$
    $$V = \pi \int_{0}^{4} \left[ (9 + 6\sqrt{y} + y) - (9 - 6\sqrt{y} + y) \right] dy = \pi \int_{0}^{4} 12\sqrt{y} dy$$
*   Evaluate:
    $$V = 12\pi \left[ \frac{2}{3} y^{3/2} \right]_0^4 = 12\pi \left( \frac{2}{3}(4)^{3/2} \right) = 12\pi \left( \frac{16}{3} \right) = 64\pi$$

## Answer
(a) $\frac{512\pi}{15}$
(b) $\frac{832\pi}{15}$
(c) $\frac{128\pi}{3}$
(d) $64\pi$

## Check by Differentiation
For part (d): $\frac{d}{dy} \left[ 8\pi y^{3/2} \right] = 12\pi y^{1/2} = \pi \left[ (3 + \sqrt{y})^2 - (3 - \sqrt{y})^2 \right]$. Verified.
