## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the indicated axis. Use cylindrical shells.
$2x - y = 4$, $x = 0$, $y = 0$; y-axis

## Rule Used
Guidelines for Finding the Volume of a Solid of Revolution Using Cylindrical Shells 5.12

## Solution
**Step 1 — Understand the Region and set up boundaries**
The equation is $y = 2x - 4$.
The bounding lines are $x = 0$ (the y-axis) and $y = 0$ (the x-axis).
The intersection with the y-axis is $(0, -4)$. The intersection with the x-axis is $(2, 0)$.
The region is a triangle in the fourth quadrant. 

**Step 2 — Identify the components of the shell**
Revolving about the y-axis uses vertical rectangles of thickness $dx$.
The radius of the shell is $x$.
The top of the region is bounded by $y = 0$. The bottom is bounded by $y = 2x - 4$.
The altitude of the shell is the top boundary minus the bottom boundary: $0 - (2x - 4) = 4 - 2x$.
Integration bounds for $x$ are from $0$ to $2$.

**Step 3 — Set up the integral for volume**
$$V = \int_{0}^{2} 2\pi x (4 - 2x) dx$$
$$V = 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} (4x - 2x^2) dx$$

**Step 4 — Evaluate the definite integral**
$$V = 2\pi \left[ 2x^2 - \frac{2x^3}{3} \right]_0^2$$
$$V = 2\pi \left( 2(2)^2 - \frac{2(2)^3}{3} \right)$$
$$V = 2\pi \left( 8 - \frac{16}{3} \right)$$
$$V = 2\pi \left( \frac{24}{3} - \frac{16}{3} \right) = 2\pi \left( \frac{8}{3} \right) = \frac{16\pi}{3}$$

## Answer
$\frac{16\pi}{3}$

## Check by Differentiation
$\frac{d}{dx} \left[ 2\pi \left( 2x^2 - \frac{2x^3}{3} \right) \right] = 2\pi \left( 4x - 2x^2 \right) = 2\pi x(4 - 2x)$. Verified.
