## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the indicated axis. Use cylindrical shells.
$y = \sqrt{x+4}$, $y = 0$, $x = 0$; x-axis

## Rule Used
Guidelines for Finding the Volume of a Solid of Revolution Using Cylindrical Shells 5.12

## Solution
**Step 1 — Understand the Region**
The region is bounded by $y = \sqrt{x+4}$, the x-axis ($y = 0$), and the y-axis ($x = 0$).
Setting $y = 0 \implies \sqrt{x+4} = 0 \implies x = -4$.
Setting $x = 0 \implies y = \sqrt{4} = 2$.
The region lies in the second quadrant, bounded by $x = -4$ to the left and $x = 0$ to the right.

**Step 2 — Identify the components of the shell**
Revolving around the x-axis utilizing cylindrical shells requires horizontal rectangles of thickness $dy$.
The radius of the shell is $y$.
To find the altitude, we rewrite $y = \sqrt{x+4}$ in terms of $y$:
$$y^2 = x + 4 \implies x = y^2 - 4$$
The right boundary is the y-axis ($x = 0$) and the left boundary is the curve $x = y^2 - 4$.
$$\text{altitude} = 0 - (y^2 - 4) = 4 - y^2$$
The limits of integration for $y$ are from $y = 0$ to $y = 2$.

**Step 3 — Set up the integral for volume**
$$V = \int_{0}^{2} 2\pi y (4 - y^2) dy$$
$$V = 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} (4y - y^3) dy$$

**Step 4 — Evaluate the definite integral**
$$V = 2\pi \left[ 2y^2 - \frac{y^4}{4} \right]_0^2$$
$$V = 2\pi \left( 2(2)^2 - \frac{2^4}{4} \right)$$
$$V = 2\pi \left( 8 - \frac{16}{4} \right)$$
$$V = 2\pi (8 - 4) = 8\pi$$

## Answer
$8\pi$

## Check by Differentiation
$\frac{d}{dy} \left[ 2\pi \left( 2y^2 - \frac{y^4}{4} \right) \right] = 2\pi (4y - y^3) = 2\pi y(4 - y^2)$. Verified.
