## Problem
Sketch the region bounded by the graphs of the equations and find its area: 
$y = x^3$; $y = x^2$

## Rule Used
Guidelines for Finding the Area of an $R_x$ Region (5.3)

## Solution
**Step 1 — Find the points of intersection**
Set equations equal to each other:
$$x^3 = x^2$$
$$x^3 - x^2 = 0$$
$$x^2(x - 1) = 0$$
The intersection points are at $x = 0$ and $x = 1$.

**Step 2 — Identify boundaries**
On the interval $[0, 1]$, for example at $x=0.5$, $y = 0.5^2 = 0.25$ and $y = 0.5^3 = 0.125$. 
Thus, the upper boundary is $f(x) = x^2$ and the lower boundary is $g(x) = x^3$.

**Step 3 — Set up the definite integral**
$$A = \int_{0}^{1} (x^2 - x^3) dx$$

**Step 4 — Evaluate the integral**
$$A = \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} - \frac{x^4}{4} \right]_0^1$$
$$A = \left( \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4} \right) - 0 = \frac{4}{12} - \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{12}$$

## Answer
$\frac{1}{12}$

## Check by Differentiation
Verify the antiderivative:
$\frac{d}{dx} \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} - \frac{x^4}{4} \right] = x^2 - x^3$. Verified.
