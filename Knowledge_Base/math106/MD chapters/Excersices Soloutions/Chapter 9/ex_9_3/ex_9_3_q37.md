## Problem
Find an equation in $x$ and $y$ that has the same graph as the polar equation and use it to help sketch the graph in an $xy$-plane.
$r \cos \theta = 5$

## Rule Used
Relationships between Rectangular and Polar Coordinates 9.9

## Solution
**Step 1 — Apply substitution**
According to the relationships between coordinate systems, $x = r \cos \theta$.
Substitute $x$ directly into the equation:
$$x = 5$$

**Step 2 — Describe the sketch**
The equation $x = 5$ defines a vertical line crossing the x-axis at a distance of 5 units to the right of the origin.

## Answer
Cartesian equation: $x = 5$. The graph is a vertical line $5$ units to the right of the y-axis.
