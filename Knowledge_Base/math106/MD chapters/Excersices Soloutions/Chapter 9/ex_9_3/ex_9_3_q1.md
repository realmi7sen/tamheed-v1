## Problem
Sketch the graph of the polar equation.
$r = 5$

## Rule Used
Graph of a Polar Equation

## Solution
**Step 1 — Analyze the equation**
The equation $r = 5$ states that the directed distance from the pole is constantly $5$, regardless of the angle $\theta$.

**Step 2 — Identify the geometric shape**
A set of points at a constant distance from a fixed central point forms a circle. Therefore, this graph is a circle centered at the pole (origin) with a radius of $5$.

## Answer
The graph is a circle of radius $5$ centered at the pole.

## Check by Differentiation
Convert to rectangular coordinates: $r^2 = 25 \implies x^2 + y^2 = 25$, confirming it is a circle of radius $5$. Verified.
