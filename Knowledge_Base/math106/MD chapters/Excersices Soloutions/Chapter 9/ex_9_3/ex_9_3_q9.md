## Problem
Sketch the graph of the polar equation.
$r = -2 \sin \theta$

## Rule Used
Relationships between Rectangular and Polar Coordinates 9.9

## Solution
**Step 1 — Convert to rectangular coordinates**
Multiply both sides of the equation by $r$:
$$r^2 = -2r \sin \theta$$

**Step 2 — Substitute rectangular equivalents**
$$x^2 + y^2 = -2y$$

**Step 3 — Complete the square**
$$x^2 + y^2 + 2y = 0$$
Add $1$ to both sides to complete the square for $y$:
$$x^2 + (y^2 + 2y + 1) = 1$$
$$x^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 1^2$$

**Step 4 — Identify the shape**
The equation represents a circle of radius $1$ centered at $(0, -1)$. The circle is tangent to the x-axis at the pole.

## Answer
The graph is a circle of radius $1$ centered at $(0, -1)$ in the Cartesian plane.
