## Problem
Find a polar equation that has the same graph as the equation in $x$ and $y$.
$2y = -x$

## Rule Used
Relationships between Rectangular and Polar Coordinates 9.9

## Solution
**Step 1 — Substitute polar relationships for $x$ and $y$**
Using $x = r \cos \theta$ and $y = r \sin \theta$:
$$2(r \sin \theta) = -(r \cos \theta)$$

**Step 2 — Solve for a trigonometric relationship**
Divide both sides by $r \cos \theta$ (assuming $r \ne 0$ and $\cos \theta \ne 0$):
$$\frac{2r \sin \theta}{r \cos \theta} = -1$$
$$2 \tan \theta = -1$$
$$\tan \theta = -\frac{1}{2}$$

**Step 3 — Isolate $\theta$**
$$\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)$$

## Answer
$\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)$
