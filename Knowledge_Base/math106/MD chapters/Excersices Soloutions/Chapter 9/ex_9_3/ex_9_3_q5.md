## Problem
Sketch the graph of the polar equation.
$r = 4 - 4 \sin \theta$

## Rule Used
Graph of a Polar Equation / Symmetry Tests

## Solution
**Step 1 — Test for symmetry**
Substitute $\pi - \theta$ for $\theta$:
$$r = 4 - 4 \sin(\pi - \theta) = 4 - 4 \sin \theta$$
Because the equation remains unchanged, the graph is symmetric with respect to the vertical line $\theta = \pi/2$ (the y-axis).

**Step 2 — Identify the shape**
The equation is of the form $r = a(1 - \sin \theta)$ where $a = 4$. This represents a cardioid pointing downwards along the vertical axis.

**Step 3 — Find key points**
- $\theta = 0 \implies r = 4 - 0 = 4 \quad \rightarrow (4, 0)$
- $\theta = \pi/2 \implies r = 4 - 4(1) = 0 \quad \rightarrow (0, \pi/2)$ (the pole)
- $\theta = \pi \implies r = 4 - 0 = 4 \quad \rightarrow (4, \pi)$
- $\theta = 3\pi/2 \implies r = 4 - 4(-1) = 8 \quad \rightarrow (8, 3\pi/2)$

**Step 4 — Sketch**
Plot the key points and utilize the symmetry. The graph forms a heart-shaped curve that dips into the pole at $\theta = \pi/2$ and reaches its maximum distance from the pole ($r=8$) at $\theta = 3\pi/2$.

## Answer
The graph is a downward-pointing cardioid passing through the pole, with its furthest point at $(8, 3\pi/2)$.
