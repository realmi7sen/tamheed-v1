## Problem
(a) Find the points on the curve $C$ at which the tangent line is either horizontal or vertical. 
(b) Find $d^2y/dx^2$. 
(c) Sketch the graph of $C$.
$x = t^3 + 1, y = t^2 - 2t$; $t$ in $\mathbb{R}$

## Rule Used
Theorem 9.4 / Second Derivative in Parametric Form 9.5

## Solution
**Part (a) — Horizontal and Vertical Tangents**
**Step 1 — Find $dy/dt$ and $dx/dt$**
$$\frac{dx}{dt} = 3t^2$$
$$\frac{dy}{dt} = 2t - 2$$

**Step 2 — Find horizontal tangents**
Set $dy/dt = 0$ (while $dx/dt \ne 0$):
$$2t - 2 = 0 \implies t = 1$$
Point: 
- At $t=1$: $x = 2, y = -1 \rightarrow (2, -1)$

**Step 3 — Find vertical tangents**
Set $dx/dt = 0$ (while $dy/dt \ne 0$):
$$3t^2 = 0 \implies t = 0$$
Point: 
- At $t=0$: $x = 1, y = 0 \rightarrow (1, 0)$

**Part (b) — Find $d^2y/dx^2$**
**Step 4 — Find $y' = dy/dx$**
$$y' = \frac{2t - 2}{3t^2} = \frac{2}{3}t^{-1} - \frac{2}{3}t^{-2}$$

**Step 5 — Apply the second derivative formula**
$$\frac{dy'}{dt} = -\frac{2}{3}t^{-2} + \frac{4}{3}t^{-3} = \frac{-2t + 4}{3t^3}$$
$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{dy'/dt}{dx/dt} = \frac{\frac{4 - 2t}{3t^3}}{3t^2} = \frac{4 - 2t}{9t^5}$$

**Part (c) — Sketching**
The curve crosses $(1,0)$ with a vertical tangent, dipping to a minimum at $(2,-1)$ with a horizontal tangent, and continuing up and to the right.

## Answer
(a) Horizontal: $(2, -1)$. Vertical: $(1, 0)$
(b) $\frac{4 - 2t}{9t^5}$
(c) Curve shaped generally like a rotated parabola with a vertical tangent cusp-like bend.
