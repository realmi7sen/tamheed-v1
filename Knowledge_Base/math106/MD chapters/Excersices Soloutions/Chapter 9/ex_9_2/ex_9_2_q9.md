## Problem
Let $C$ be the curve with the given parametrization, for $t$ in $\mathbb{R}$. Find the points on $C$ at which the slope of the tangent line is $m$.
$x = -t^3, y = -6t^2 - 18t$; $m = 2$

## Rule Used
Theorem 9.4 (Derivative of Parametric Equations)

## Solution
**Step 1 — Find $dy/dx$**
$$\frac{dx}{dt} = -3t^2$$
$$\frac{dy}{dt} = -12t - 18$$
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{-12t - 18}{-3t^2} = \frac{4t + 6}{t^2}$$

**Step 2 — Set $dy/dx = 2$ and solve for $t$**
$$\frac{4t + 6}{t^2} = 2$$
$$4t + 6 = 2t^2$$
$$2t^2 - 4t - 6 = 0$$
$$t^2 - 2t - 3 = 0$$
$$(t - 3)(t + 1) = 0$$
This yields $t = 3$ and $t = -1$.

**Step 3 — Find the corresponding points $(x, y)$**
For $t = 3$:
$$x(3) = -(3)^3 = -27$$
$$y(3) = -6(3)^2 - 18(3) = -54 - 54 = -108$$
Point 1: $(-27, -108)$

For $t = -1$:
$$x(-1) = -(-1)^3 = 1$$
$$y(-1) = -6(-1)^2 - 18(-1) = -6 + 18 = 12$$
Point 2: $(1, 12)$

## Answer
$(-27, -108)$ and $(1, 12)$

## Check by Differentiation
At $t=3$, $dx/dt = -27 \ne 0$. At $t=-1$, $dx/dt = -3 \ne 0$. Both points are valid. Verified.
