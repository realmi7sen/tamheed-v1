## Problem
Find the area of the region that is inside the graphs of both equations.
$r = \sin \theta$, $r = \sqrt{3} \cos \theta$

## Rule Used
Theorem 9.12 (Area of a Polar Region)

## Solution
**Step 1 — Analyze the graphs and find intersections**
$r = \sin \theta$ is a circle sitting above the polar axis. $r = \sqrt{3} \cos \theta$ is a circle sitting to the right of the y-axis. The region common to both lies in the first quadrant.
Find the intersection point:
$$\sin \theta = \sqrt{3} \cos \theta$$
$$\tan \theta = \sqrt{3}$$
$$\theta = \pi/3$$
The common region is bounded by $r = \sin \theta$ from $\theta = 0$ to $\pi/3$, and by $r = \sqrt{3} \cos \theta$ from $\theta = \pi/3$ to $\pi/2$.

**Step 2 — Set up the integrals**
The total common area is the sum of two pieces:
$$A = \int_{0}^{\pi/3} \frac{1}{2} (\sin \theta)^2 d\theta + \int_{\pi/3}^{\pi/2} \frac{1}{2} (\sqrt{3} \cos \theta)^2 d\theta$$
$$A = \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{\pi/3} \sin^2 \theta d\theta + \frac{3}{2} \int_{\pi/3}^{\pi/2} \cos^2 \theta d\theta$$

**Step 3 — Use trigonometric identities**
Use the half-angle formulas:
$$A = \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{\pi/3} \left( \frac{1 - \cos 2\theta}{2} \right) d\theta + \frac{3}{2} \int_{\pi/3}^{\pi/2} \left( \frac{1 + \cos 2\theta}{2} \right) d\theta$$
$$A = \frac{1}{4} \left[ \theta - \frac{1}{2} \sin 2\theta \right]_0^{\pi/3} + \frac{3}{4} \left[ \theta + \frac{1}{2} \sin 2\theta \right]_{\pi/3}^{\pi/2}$$

**Step 4 — Evaluate the integrals**
First piece:
$$\frac{1}{4} \left( \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{2} \sin\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) \right) - 0 = \frac{1}{4} \left( \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \right) = \frac{\pi}{12} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{16}$$
Second piece:
$$\frac{3}{4} \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + 0 \right) - \frac{3}{4} \left( \frac{\pi}{3} + \frac{1}{2} \sin\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) \right) = \frac{3\pi}{8} - \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{3}{4}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right) = \frac{\pi}{8} - \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{16}$$

Sum the pieces:
$$A = \left( \frac{\pi}{12} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{16} \right) + \left( \frac{\pi}{8} - \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{16} \right)$$
$$A = \left( \frac{2\pi}{24} + \frac{3\pi}{24} \right) - \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{16} = \frac{5\pi}{24} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$$

## Answer
$\frac{5\pi}{24} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$
