## Problem
Find an equation in $x$ and $y$ that has the same graph as the polar equation and use it to help sketch the graph in an $xy$-plane.
$r \sin \theta = -2$

## Rule Used
Relationships between Rectangular and Polar Coordinates 9.9

## Solution
**Step 1 — Apply substitution**
According to the coordinate relationships, $y = r \sin \theta$.
Substitute $y$ directly into the equation:
$$y = -2$$

**Step 2 — Describe the sketch**
The equation $y = -2$ defines a horizontal line crossing the y-axis at a distance of 2 units below the origin.

## Answer
Cartesian equation: $y = -2$. The graph is a horizontal line $2$ units below the x-axis.
