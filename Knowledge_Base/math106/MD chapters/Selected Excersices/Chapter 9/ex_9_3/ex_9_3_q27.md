## Problem
Find a polar equation that has the same graph as the equation in $x$ and $y$.
$x = -3$

## Rule Used
Relationships between Rectangular and Polar Coordinates 9.9

## Solution
**Step 1 — Substitute polar relationship for $x$**
We know that $x = r \cos \theta$.
$$r \cos \theta = -3$$

**Step 2 — Solve for $r$**
Divide both sides by $\cos \theta$:
$$r = \frac{-3}{\cos \theta}$$
$$r = -3 \sec \theta$$

## Answer
$r = -3 \sec \theta$
