## Problem
Find a polar equation that has the same graph as the equation in $x$ and $y$.
$y^2 - x^2 = 4$

## Rule Used
Relationships between Rectangular and Polar Coordinates 9.9

## Solution
**Step 1 — Substitute polar relationships**
Replace $x$ with $r \cos \theta$ and $y$ with $r \sin \theta$:
$$(r \sin \theta)^2 - (r \cos \theta)^2 = 4$$
$$r^2 \sin^2 \theta - r^2 \cos^2 \theta = 4$$

**Step 2 — Factor and apply trigonometric identities**
Factor out $-r^2$:
$$-r^2 (\cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta) = 4$$
Apply the double-angle identity for cosine ($\cos 2\theta = \cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta$):
$$-r^2 \cos 2\theta = 4$$

**Step 3 — Solve for $r^2$**
$$r^2 = -\frac{4}{\cos 2\theta}$$
$$r^2 = -4 \sec 2\theta$$

## Answer
$r^2 = -4 \sec 2\theta$
