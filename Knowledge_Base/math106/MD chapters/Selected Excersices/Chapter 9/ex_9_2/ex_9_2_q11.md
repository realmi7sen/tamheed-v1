## Problem
(a) Find the points on the curve $C$ at which the tangent line is either horizontal or vertical. 
(b) Find $d^2y/dx^2$. 
(c) Sketch the graph of $C$.
$x = 4t^2, y = t^3 - 12t$; $t$ in $\mathbb{R}$

## Rule Used
Theorem 9.4 / Second Derivative in Parametric Form 9.5

## Solution
**Part (a) — Horizontal and Vertical Tangents**
**Step 1 — Find $dy/dt$ and $dx/dt$**
$$\frac{dx}{dt} = 8t$$
$$\frac{dy}{dt} = 3t^2 - 12$$

**Step 2 — Find horizontal tangents**
Set $dy/dt = 0$ (while $dx/dt \ne 0$):
$$3t^2 - 12 = 0 \implies t^2 = 4 \implies t = 2, t = -2$$
Points: 
- At $t=2$: $x = 16, y = -16 \rightarrow (16, -16)$
- At $t=-2$: $x = 16, y = 16 \rightarrow (16, 16)$

**Step 3 — Find vertical tangents**
Set $dx/dt = 0$ (while $dy/dt \ne 0$):
$$8t = 0 \implies t = 0$$
Point: 
- At $t=0$: $x = 0, y = 0 \rightarrow (0, 0)$

**Part (b) — Find $d^2y/dx^2$**
**Step 4 — Find $y' = dy/dx$**
$$y' = \frac{3t^2 - 12}{8t} = \frac{3}{8}t - \frac{3}{2}t^{-1}$$

**Step 5 — Apply the second derivative formula**
$$\frac{dy'}{dt} = \frac{3}{8} + \frac{3}{2}t^{-2} = \frac{3}{8} + \frac{12}{8t^2} = \frac{3t^2 + 12}{8t^2}$$
$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{dy'/dt}{dx/dt} = \frac{\frac{3t^2 + 12}{8t^2}}{8t} = \frac{3t^2 + 12}{64t^3}$$

**Part (c) — Sketching**
The curve crosses the origin with a vertical tangent. It moves to the right, peaking at $(16, 16)$ and dipping to $(16, -16)$, forming a sideways loop. (Sketch omitted per format constraints, but key features identified).

## Answer
(a) Horizontal: $(16, -16)$ and $(16, 16)$. Vertical: $(0, 0)$
(b) $\frac{3t^2 + 12}{64t^3}$
(c) Sideways loop opening to the right, crossing at origin.
