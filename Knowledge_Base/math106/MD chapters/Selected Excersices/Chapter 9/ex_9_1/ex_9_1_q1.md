## Problem
(a) Find an equation in $x$ and $y$ whose graph contains the points on the curve $C$. 
(b) Sketch the graph of $C$ and indicate the orientation.
$x = t - 2$, $y = 2t + 3$; $0 \le t \le 5$

## Rule Used
Eliminating the parameter

## Solution
**Step 1 — Solve for $t$ in terms of $x$**
From the first parametric equation:
$$x = t - 2 \implies t = x + 2$$

**Step 2 — Substitute into the $y$ equation**
Substitute $t = x + 2$ into the equation for $y$:
$$y = 2(x + 2) + 3$$
$$y = 2x + 4 + 3$$
$$y = 2x + 7$$

**Step 3 — Determine the domain and endpoints**
The parameter is restricted to $0 \le t \le 5$. 
Find the starting point (at $t = 0$):
$$x(0) = 0 - 2 = -2$$
$$y(0) = 2(0) + 3 = 3$$
Start point: $(-2, 3)$.

Find the ending point (at $t = 5$):
$$x(5) = 5 - 2 = 3$$
$$y(5) = 2(5) + 3 = 13$$
End point: $(3, 13)$.

Since the relationship between $x$ and $t$ is linear and strictly increasing, $x$ ranges continuously from $-2$ to $3$. The curve is a line segment.

## Answer
(a) $y = 2x + 7$
(b) The graph is a straight line segment starting at $(-2, 3)$ and ending at $(3, 13)$. The orientation is upward and to the right.

## Check by Differentiation
Substitute $x(t)$ and $y(t)$ into the Cartesian equation to verify the identity:
$2(t - 2) + 7 = 2t - 4 + 7 = 2t + 3$. This matches $y(t)$. Verified.
