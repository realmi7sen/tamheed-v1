## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the indicated axis.
$y = 1/x$, $x = 1$, $x = 3$, $y = 0$; x-axis

## Rule Used
Volume $V$ of a Circular Disk 5.7

## Solution
**Step 1 — Identify the components of the disk**
Because the region is revolved around the x-axis, the cross-sections perpendicular to the axis of revolution are circular disks.
The radius of a typical disk is the distance from the x-axis to the curve: $\text{radius} = f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$.
The thickness of the disk is $dx$.

**Step 2 — Set up the integral for volume**
The bounds are given by the vertical lines $x = 1$ and $x = 3$.
$$V = \int_{1}^{3} \pi [f(x)]^2 dx$$
$$V = \int_{1}^{3} \pi \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 dx$$
$$V = \pi \int_{1}^{3} x^{-2} dx$$

**Step 3 — Evaluate the definite integral**
$$V = \pi \left[ \frac{x^{-1}}{-1} \right]_1^3$$
$$V = \pi \left[ -\frac{1}{x} \right]_1^3$$
$$V = \pi \left( -\frac{1}{3} - \left(-\frac{1}{1}\right) \right)$$
$$V = \pi \left( -\frac{1}{3} + 1 \right) = \frac{2\pi}{3}$$

## Answer
$\frac{2\pi}{3}$

## Check by Differentiation
$\frac{d}{dx} \left[ -\pi x^{-1} \right] = \pi x^{-2} = \pi \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2$. Verified.
