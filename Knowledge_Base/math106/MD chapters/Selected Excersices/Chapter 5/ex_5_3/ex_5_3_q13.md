## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the indicated axis. Use cylindrical shells.
$x^2 = 4y$, $y = 4$; x-axis

## Rule Used
Guidelines for Finding the Volume of a Solid of Revolution Using Cylindrical Shells 5.12

## Solution
**Step 1 — Choose the appropriate slicing direction**
Because we are revolving around the x-axis and using the cylindrical shell method, we must use horizontal rectangles with thickness $dy$.

**Step 2 — Identify the components of the shell**
The radius of the shell is the distance to the x-axis, which is $y$.
Solving the curve for $x$ gives the left and right boundaries: $x = \pm 2\sqrt{y}$.
The right boundary is $x = 2\sqrt{y}$ and the left boundary is $x = -2\sqrt{y}$.
The altitude (length) of the shell is the right boundary minus the left boundary:
$$\text{altitude} = 2\sqrt{y} - (-2\sqrt{y}) = 4\sqrt{y}$$
The bounds of integration along the y-axis are from the vertex $y = 0$ to the line $y = 4$.

**Step 3 — Set up the integral for volume**
$$V = \int_{0}^{4} 2\pi y (4\sqrt{y}) dy$$
$$V = 8\pi \int_{0}^{4} y^{3/2} dy$$

**Step 4 — Evaluate the definite integral**
$$V = 8\pi \left[ \frac{2}{5} y^{5/2} \right]_0^4$$
$$V = 8\pi \left( \frac{2}{5} (4)^{5/2} \right)$$
$$V = 8\pi \left( \frac{2}{5} \cdot 32 \right)$$
$$V = \frac{512\pi}{5}$$

## Answer
$\frac{512\pi}{5}$

## Check by Differentiation
$\frac{d}{dy} \left[ 8\pi \left(\frac{2}{5}\right) y^{5/2} \right] = 8\pi y^{3/2} = 2\pi y (4\sqrt{y})$. Verified.
