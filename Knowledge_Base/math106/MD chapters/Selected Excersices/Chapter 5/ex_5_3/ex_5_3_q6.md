## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the indicated axis. Use cylindrical shells.
$y = 1/x$, $x = 1$, $x = 2$, $y = 0$; y-axis

## Rule Used
Guidelines for Finding the Volume of a Solid of Revolution Using Cylindrical Shells 5.12

## Solution
**Step 1 — Identify the components of the shell**
Revolving about the y-axis uses vertical rectangles of thickness $dx$.
The average radius of the shell is $x$.
The altitude of the shell is $y = \frac{1}{x}$.
The integration bounds are $x = 1$ to $x = 2$.

**Step 2 — Set up the integral for volume**
$$V = \int_{1}^{2} 2\pi x \left(\frac{1}{x}\right) dx$$
$$V = 2\pi \int_{1}^{2} 1 dx$$

**Step 3 — Evaluate the definite integral**
$$V = 2\pi \left[ x \right]_1^2$$
$$V = 2\pi (2 - 1)$$
$$V = 2\pi$$

## Answer
$2\pi$

## Check by Differentiation
$\frac{d}{dx} \left[ 2\pi x \right] = 2\pi = 2\pi x \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$. Verified.
