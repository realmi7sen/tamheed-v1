## Problem
Sketch the region $R$ bounded by the graphs of the equations, and find the volume of the solid generated if $R$ is revolved about the indicated axis. Use cylindrical shells.
$y = \sqrt{x}$, $x = 4$, $y = 0$; y-axis

## Rule Used
Guidelines for Finding the Volume of a Solid of Revolution Using Cylindrical Shells 5.12

## Solution
**Step 1 — Identify the components of the shell**
Revolving a region defined by $x$ about the y-axis requires vertical rectangles of thickness $dx$.
The average radius of the shell is the distance from the y-axis to the vertical rectangle: $\text{radius} = x$.
The altitude (height) of the shell is the top curve minus the bottom curve: $\text{height} = \sqrt{x} - 0 = \sqrt{x}$.
The limits of integration for $x$ are from $x = 0$ to $x = 4$.

**Step 2 — Set up the integral for volume**
$$V = \int_{0}^{4} 2\pi x (\sqrt{x}) dx$$
$$V = 2\pi \int_{0}^{4} x^{3/2} dx$$

**Step 3 — Evaluate the definite integral**
$$V = 2\pi \left[ \frac{2}{5} x^{5/2} \right]_0^4$$
$$V = 2\pi \left( \frac{2}{5} (4)^{5/2} - 0 \right)$$
$$V = 2\pi \left( \frac{2}{5} \cdot 32 \right)$$
$$V = \frac{128\pi}{5}$$

## Answer
$\frac{128\pi}{5}$

## Check by Differentiation
$\frac{d}{dx} \left[ 2\pi \left(\frac{2}{5}\right) x^{5/2} \right] = 2\pi \left(\frac{2}{5}\right) \left(\frac{5}{2}\right) x^{3/2} = 2\pi x \sqrt{x}$. Verified.
