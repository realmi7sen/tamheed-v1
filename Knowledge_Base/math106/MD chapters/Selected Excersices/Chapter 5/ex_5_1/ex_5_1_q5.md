## Problem
Sketch the region bounded by the graphs of the equations and find its area: 
$y = x^2$; $y = 4x$

## Rule Used
Guidelines for Finding the Area of an $R_x$ Region (5.3)

## Solution
**Step 1 — Find the points of intersection**
Set the two equations equal to each other to find the intersection boundaries for the region:
$$x^2 = 4x$$
$$x^2 - 4x = 0$$
$$x(x - 4) = 0$$
The intersections are at $x = 0$ and $x = 4$.

**Step 2 — Identify the upper and lower boundaries**
On the interval $[0, 4]$, test a point (e.g., $x=2$) to see which function is on top:
$y = 4(2) = 8$ and $y = 2^2 = 4$.
Thus, $f(x) = 4x$ is the upper boundary and $g(x) = x^2$ is the lower boundary.

**Step 3 — Set up the definite integral**
$$A = \int_{0}^{4} [4x - x^2] dx$$

**Step 4 — Evaluate the integral**
$$A = \left[ 2x^2 - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^4$$
$$A = \left( 2(4)^2 - \frac{4^3}{3} \right) - (0)$$
$$A = 32 - \frac{64}{3} = \frac{96}{3} - \frac{64}{3} = \frac{32}{3}$$

## Answer
$\frac{32}{3}$

## Check by Differentiation
Verify the antiderivative:
$\frac{d}{dx} \left[ 2x^2 - \frac{x^3}{3} \right] = 4x - 3\left(\frac{x^2}{3}\right) = 4x - x^2$. Verified.
